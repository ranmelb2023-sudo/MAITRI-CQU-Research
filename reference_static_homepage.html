About MAITRI

Intelligent inspection for smarter solar infrastructure.

The research proposes a secure and intelligent autonomous drone-based monitoring system powered by Edge-AI for real-time fault detection in solar energy infrastructure.

The framework brings together drone-based RGB and thermal imaging, lightweight AI models, explainability, hardware-aware optimisation and cyber resilience.

Research objectives

Six objectives guiding the project.

01

Autonomous inspection

Develop an autonomous drone-based solar inspection platform using RGB and thermal imaging sensors.

02

Lightweight Edge-AI

Design computationally efficient models for real-time onboard fault detection and localisation.

03

Explainable AI

Integrate XAI techniques to improve interpretability and trustworthiness of AI-based decisions.

04

Security & resilience

Develop secure communication and resilient AI mechanisms against cyber threats and adversarial manipulation.

05

Hardware-aware optimisation

Optimise AI inference performance for embedded GPU and FPGA-enabled systems.

06

Validation

Validate the framework using benchmark datasets, simulation environments and real-time drone experimentation.

Research methodology

From system design to evaluation.

🚁
1. Data CollectionDrone RGB and thermal imagery; public solar fault datasets
🧠
2. Model DevelopmentCNN / YOLO-based lightweight models; fault classification and localisation
⚡
3. OptimisationQuantisation, pruning and knowledge distillation
▣
4. Edge DeploymentNVIDIA Jetson / FPGA implementation and real-time inference
✓
5. EvaluationAccuracy, precision, recall, F1, latency and energy efficiency

Research questions

Questions guiding the investigation.

RQ1 How can drone systems be integrated with Edge-AI for real-time solar fault detection?
RQ2 What lightweight AI models can achieve high accuracy under embedded hardware constraints?
RQ3 How can explainability improve trust in AI-based solar inspection systems?
RQ4 How can cyber resilience be improved in autonomous drone systems?
RQ5 Can real-time onboard inference outperform cloud-based systems in latency and efficiency?
Research roadmap

Three years of progressive development.

Year 1

Foundations
  • Literature review
  • Dataset preparation
  • Baseline AI model development
  • Review and baseline publications

Year 2

Edge intelligence
  • Edge-AI optimisation
  • XAI integration
  • Initial drone-based deployment
  • Real-time detection research

Year 3

Secure deployment
  • Secure and robust drone systems
  • Full real-time embedded deployment
  • Real-world validation
  • Benchmarking and publications
